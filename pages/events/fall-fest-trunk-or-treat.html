THES PTA Fall Fest & Trunk or Treat 2026

An evening of fall fun, food trucks, games, and trick-or-treating for the whole family.

WhenFriday, October 30, 2026 · 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
WhereThunder Hill Elementary School
9357 Mellenbrook Road, Columbia, MD 21045 · Howard County
Questions?president.thespta@gmail.com
Add to Google Calendar View Flyer

Join us for a free evening of fall fun for the whole Thunder Hill family! Fall Fest kicks things off with music, games, and festive activities, followed by Trunk or Treat, where families decorate their cars and hand out treats in the school parking lot.

This is a free THES PTA event — everyone is welcome.

What to Expect

  • Fall Fest, 5:00 – 6:30 PM: music, festive fall activities, games, and contests
  • Food trucks, raffles, and much more
  • Trunk or Treat, 6:30 – 8:00 PM
  • Rain date: Sunday, November 1, 1:00 – 4:00 PM

Good to Know

  • Children must be accompanied by an adult
  • Parking will be limited
  • No scary or gory costumes, and no face mask coverings
  • Donate candy for Trunk or Treat October 12 – 30 in the Candy Robot box in the THES Main Office

Volunteer or Host a Trunk

  • Help run Fall Fest activities
  • Sign up your vehicle to decorate a trunk and hand out treats
